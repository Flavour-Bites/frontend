## 🍪 **Project Proposal for Flavour Bites Website**

### 1. Introduction

Flavour Bites is a home-based cake and cookies business that brings sweet homemade treats to customers who appreciate freshness and flavor.
To make ordering easier and to reach more people, we’ll create a simple and attractive website where customers can browse products, place orders, and contact Flavour Bites directly.

---

### 2. Project Goal

The main goal of this project is to help Flavour Bites grow online by:

* Making it easy for customers to order cakes and cookies.
* Showing the variety of baked goods with clear photos and descriptions.
* Allowing the business to receive and manage orders smoothly.

---

### 3. Target Users

* Local customers who love homemade cakes and cookies.
* People who want to order for birthdays, small events, or gifts.

---

### 4. Website Features (First Version)

The website will include:

1. **Home Page** – A welcoming introduction and photos of delicious treats.
2. **Product Page** – Display of cakes, cookies, and other baked goods with images and prices.
3. **Order Form** – A simple form for customers to place orders (name, phone, product, message).
4. **Contact Page** – WhatsApp button or message form to reach Flavour Bites easily.
5. **Mobile-Friendly Design** – The website will look good on both phones and computers.

---

### 5. Future Features (Later Upgrades)

In the future, we can add:

* Online payments (for card or mobile money).
* Order tracking.
* Customer accounts and order history.
* Reviews and ratings.
* A dashboard for managing products and orders.

---

### 6. Technology Plan

The website will be built using modern and reliable technologies:

* **Frontend:** React + Tailwind CSS (for a fast, beautiful design)
* **Backend:** Django (for handling orders and managing data)
* **Database:** PostgreSQL (to store products and orders)
* **Hosting:** Free or low-cost platforms like Vercel and Render

This setup will make the website fast, secure, and ready to grow when Flavour Bites expands.

---

### 8. Deliverables

At the end of the project, Flavour Bites will receive:

* A fully working and hosted website.
* Access to manage product information.
* Short guide on how to handle small updates (like changing prices or pictures).

---

### 9. Costs (for now)

Website development is free since it’s a personal project.
However, future domain name and hosting costs might apply (for example:

* **Domain:** around 800–1,000 ETB/year
* **Hosting:** can start free, then upgrade later if traffic grows).

---

### 10. Conclusion

This project will help **Flavour Bites** reach more customers, accept online orders easily, and build a stronger presence in the local market.
It’s the first step toward turning Flavour Bites into a modern, recognizable baking brand.
